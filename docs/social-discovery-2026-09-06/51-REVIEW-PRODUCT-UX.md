## What is genuinely good

- The privacy line holds where it matters. `PostCard.bodyForKind` names every field
  one at a time with no payload spread; `ProgrammeStructure` reads no load field;
  `PrivacyReceipt` is honest in both columns; `community.privacy.guard` passes.
- `transport.js` gates in the right order and fails **closed** on unresolved
  Article 9 consent, with a tri-state session check that does not switch
  Community off for an unreadable Keychain.
- SD-04 is respected at the server boundary: `search_people`, `search_programmes`,
  `discover_*`, `get_profile`, `get_programme` and `get_post` all work without a
  profile. Only the client's `Promise.all` throws that away (finding 3).
- `CommunityAdaptScreen` treats an unreadable capability state as "not checked",
  not "no restrictions", and refuses to save. That is the correct hard call.
- Visual ruling 3 is followed exactly: one "Respect" tap, `thumbs-up-outline` →
  `thumbs-up`, `chatbubble-outline`, no hearts. Ruling 1 too: suggestion reasons
  and the circuit label are `textPrimary`; no amber body text anywhere in `src/`.
- One `emphatic` button per screen, and never on the programme screen — ruling 2
  landed as written.
- Small-network honesty is real: Volyume tiles carry both "By Volyume" and the
  Volyume chip, dimensions are "6 people" rather than a community, and nothing
  pads a list.
- Nothing Community-related appears in `ActiveWorkoutScreen`, the rest timer, or
  a read-only summary re-open. Training is uninterrupted.

---

## Verdict

**Do not merge.** Three of the four P0s are shipped-and-broken user paths, not
polish: profile editing and the privacy visibility control cannot succeed at all
(a missing `handle` against a replace-semantics upsert), and every user who has
not joined sees an empty Discover plus a false "You are early". The fourth is a
red tree. The architecture, the privacy discipline and the visual work are sound
and worth keeping; fix findings 1-11, re-run the device checklist steps 1, 5, 11
and 14, and this is a strong first version.
