# Research: Strava & Garmin Connect social layers (2025-2026)

Web access worked for this research; all findings below are sourced unless
marked "unverified". Current as of search date 2026-09-06; Garmin's
follower-system rebuild was mid-rollout at time of writing (leaked/beta
signal, not fully GA everywhere) - flagged inline.

## STRAVA

### Clubs
- Any athlete can create a club for any of Strava's supported sport types; clubs live under the app's "Groups" tab. [Clubs on Strava](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918347-Clubs-on-Strava)
- Club owners/admins create and edit club Events; depending on settings, members can post to the club feed. [Group Events for Clubs](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918607-Group-Events-for-Clubs)
- Leaderboards: single-sport clubs rank by total distance; multisport clubs rank by total time. Clubs with <=500 members show a weekly top-10 scatterplot; the website shows weekly top-100. [endurance.biz](https://endurance.biz/2026/industry-news/strava-targets-leaderboard-accuracy-and-rolls-out-navigation-and-club-event-updates/)
- 2026 additions: an Events sub-tab inside Groups surfacing group runs/rides/races by activity history + location, plus a redesigned Club Organiser Hub and a Races Hub directory. [endurance.biz](https://endurance.biz/2026/industry-news/strava-introduces-race-and-club-event-discovery-features/)
- Moderation: admins can approve join requests, promote/demote admins (not the owner), and remove members; mobile does this via a three-dot menu per member. Admins are held to Strava's Acceptable Use Policy / Community Standards and expected to use in-app reporting. [Club Moderation Guidelines](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/27363926037261-Club-Moderation-Guidelines-For-Admins-and-Owners); [Clubs on the Mobile App](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/221622188-Clubs-on-the-Mobile-App)
- Requested-but-missing: bulk member removal is an open community feature request, not shipped. [Community idea thread](https://communityhub.strava.com/t5/ideas/add-the-ability-for-club-admins-to-remove-multiple-club-members/idi-p/4808)
- Verified Club badge: earned (not paid), requires owner/admin using real name + clear photo, 18+, club must be active with >=100 members and >=1 post/week, and have a recognised name/brand with positive community impact. Verified clubs get a checkmark and are surfaced as recommended clubs. [Verified Badge for Clubs](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401828-verified-badge-for-clubs-on-strava)
- Leaderboard integrity: Strava reprocessed top-100 ride leaderboards with improved e-bike detection, removing 3.9M activities (2.3M mis-tagged e-bike rides) - shows leaderboard trust needs active anti-cheat work, not just a display feature. [endurance.biz](https://endurance.biz/2026/industry-news/strava-targets-leaderboard-accuracy-and-rolls-out-navigation-and-club-event-updates/)
- Not found in public docs: an explicit minimum-member threshold or automatic handling for empty/dormant clubs; only the Verified badge sets a hard membership floor. Unverified beyond that.

### Local
- Segments: user-defined road/trail sections; the map lets you zoom/pan or search a location to surface the most popular segments there, filterable by sport, length, surface, flat/steep/hilly. [road.cc](https://road.cc/content/feature/how-make-most-stravas-exploring-features-286065); [Strava Segments](https://www.strava.com/maps/segments)
- Local Legend (renamed from historic "King/Queen of the Mountain" framing): awarded to whoever has the MOST efforts on a segment in a rolling 90-day window, not the fastest time - rewards consistency/frequency over speed, decays as old efforts age off. Separate men's/women's crown. Only counts activities set to "Everyone" visibility. Introduced July 2020, still active as of Feb 2025 Strava social posts - no evidence of removal. [DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2020/06/strava-legends-feature.html); [Local Legends help](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401751-local-legends); [Strava X post Feb 2025](https://x.com/Strava/status/1894165543633997848)
- Route discovery: personalized route suggestions from your phone; a "Go for a workout" category surfaces segments/areas where the community trains often (e.g. local tracks). Heatmap overlay shows aggregate popularity from ~3B activities worldwide. [stories.strava.com](https://stories.strava.com/articles/find-new-routes-near-you)
- **2018 heatmap incident**: the global heatmap (built from public activity data) inadvertently revealed the layout and movement patterns of secret military bases (Afghanistan, Syria, etc.) because military personnel's fitness-tracker activity was the dominant signal in otherwise-empty areas - the anomaly of *any* dense activity there was the leak, not any single user's data. [Android Police retrospective](https://www.androidpolice.com/strava-heatmaps-location-identity-doxxing-problem/); [Wired PDF](https://courses.cs.washington.edu/courses/cse490c/18au/readings/strava_wired.pdf)
- Post-incident response was criticised as thin: Strava's fix was limited to hiding the first/last segments of a route in the activity summary (Privacy Zones); the Android Police retrospective argues Strava "hasn't learned much" since, because the heatmap itself and aggregate-pattern leakage were never redesigned - responsibility shifted to governments banning app use on bases rather than Strava changing the product. [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/strava-heatmaps-location-identity-doxxing-problem/)
- Privacy Zone mechanic: user sets an address + radius (up to 1000m); any activity starting/ending inside that zone is hidden from segments/leaderboards near it and the exact address is obscured from other viewers - but the zone must be self-configured, it is not on by default. [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2019/02/21/strava-privacy-zone/); [Edit Map Visibility](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000173384-Edit-Map-Visibility)
- Separate control: "hide start/end point" toggle, independent of Privacy Zone, for any activity regardless of location. [Edit Map Visibility](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000173384-Edit-Map-Visibility)
- Continuing criticism as recently as 2023: heatmap-driven home-address exposure remained a live concern reported in tech press, i.e. the underlying pattern-inference risk was never fully solved by Privacy Zones. [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2023/6/strava-heatmaps-raise-privacy-concerns-by-exposing-home-addresses/)

### Flyby (opt-in history - directly relevant to any "who else trained near you" feature)
- Originally opt-OUT: Flyby (which showed you crossed paths with other athletes during an activity, replayable after the fact) was on by default; users had to manually disable it. [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/strava-removes-automatic-flybys-after-safety-concerns-472797)
- Safety concern that forced the change: users showed it was trivial to use Flyby to infer a stranger's regular route/schedule, and one widely shared story described a man's run being logged as "running together" with a woman he'd merely passed - raising stalking/harassment risk, especially in secluded areas. [DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2020/10/strava-flyby-feature.html)
- October 2020: Strava flipped Flyby to opt-IN for all users - default became private/off, requiring active enrolment via privacy settings to appear in others' Flybys. This is the single clearest precedent of a "who was near you" feature being reversed from opt-out to opt-in after real-world safety harm. [DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2020/10/strava-flyby-feature.html); [Flyby Privacy Controls](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401996-flyby-privacy-controls)

### People
- Profile visibility has three tiers: Everyone, Followers, Only You - set at both profile and per-activity level. [Strava Privacy Controls FAQ](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025920332-Strava-s-Privacy-Controls-FAQ)
- Follow model: default is open follow (anyone can follow you), but a profile set to "Followers" requires the follower to send a request that you approve/deny via notification - i.e. Strava supports an opt-in "approve followers" gate but does not require it. [How to approve or deny follower requests](https://communityhub.strava.com/insider-journal-9/how-to-approve-or-deny-strava-follower-requests-1501)
- Once approved, followers see: profile photo, location, bio, recent photos, last-4-weeks calendar widget, clubs, achievements, trophy case, following/followers, KOMs/QOMs/CRs/Top-10s, best-effort posts, activity bar chart, summaries, side-by-side comparisons, gear (shoes/bikes). This is a wide default disclosure surface once someone is approved. [Profile Page Privacy Controls](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401967-profile-page-privacy-controls)
- No confirmed public documentation found for a dedicated "suggested athletes" (contacts/Facebook import) feature as of this search; Strava's own help centre does not describe it explicitly in the results returned. Unverified either way - do not assert it exists or doesn't beyond that.
- Under-18 defaults (since 3 Sept 2021): profile forced to Followers-only visibility for non-approved viewers; activities never appear on segment/challenge leaderboards; no access to Messaging. Strava cannot fully prevent under-13 signups (self-reported DOB), and auto-corrects an entered age below 13 up to 13 - a soft, not hard, gate. Under-16s cannot upload or see heart-rate data (EU data-protection driven). [Privacy defaults under 18](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412328250893-Your-Privacy-Control-Defaults-When-You-re-Under-18-on-Strava); [Age and Minor Safety](https://support.strava.com/en-us/collections/19669315-age-and-minor-safety)

### Feed
- Feed Ordering is personalised (not strictly chronological), weighted toward the kinds of activities a user tends to interact with. [Feed Ordering](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15402105-feed-ordering)
- Major, repeated user complaint: the feed is saturated with automatic, un-curated activity posts pushed by watches/Peloton/third-party integrations - "every pushed activity is promoted directly to the feed" with no filtering by intent, leading one reviewer to call Strava "the least social, social platform I use." [Medium - "Strava Has a Feed Problem"](https://medium.com/@zolekker/strava-has-a-feed-problem-26a6d2a2f417)
- Automated "motivational" kudos: Strava itself (not a real follower) sometimes generates kudos-like messages ("Way to go") - widely called out in the community forum as "the single most annoying thing about the app" and, per multiple threads, not disable-able as a discrete notification setting. [Community thread](https://communityhub.strava.com/strava-features-chat-5/how-to-remove-mute-motivational-messages-from-strava-502)
- Engagement is thin even for popular athletes: most posts see well under ~1,500 kudos and only a handful of comments - kudos is a low-friction/low-signal mechanic, not deep engagement. [Harvard Digital Innovation piece](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-digit/?p=14206) (secondary/academic source, treat as illustrative not definitive)

### Challenges
- Types: distance/elevation/time goals, segment-match challenges, "active X days" streak challenges; duration from a single day to a month. [Strava Challenges](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401916-strava-challenges)
- Group Challenges (mobile-only): private, visible only to participants, built for motivation among friends rather than the public gallery. [Group Challenges](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401736-group-challenges)
- Badges land in a personal "Trophy Case," visible on web and mobile (e.g. NHS Active Challenge finisher badge). [Strava Challenges](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216919177-Strava-Challenges)
- Complaint pattern: the public Challenge gallery is substantially paid brand placement (e.g. Le Col, Brooks, Red Bull sponsor challenges) - users note the gallery reads as advertising, not organic community challenge discovery; some challenge features gate behind subscription. [Community discussion](https://communityhub.strava.com/strava-features-chat-5/challenge-related-9330)

### Live / safety-broadcast
- Strava Beacon: sends a live-tracking link to up to 3 pre-selected safety contacts when an activity starts; link updates location every ~15 seconds, shows current position, route so far, start point, and device battery %; stops automatically when the activity ends. **Contacts do NOT need a Strava account** - they get an SMS link, viewable in a mobile browser. Free on the phone app since a pricing change; still Premium-gated for use from standalone GPS devices/watches without a phone. Explicitly framed as a safety feature (launched 2016 following high-profile athlete-safety concerns). [Coach](https://www.coachweb.com/fitness-apps/9051/strava-s-beacon-safety-feature-is-now-free-to-use); [Strava Beacon help](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/224357527-Strava-Beacon); [TechCrunch 2016](https://techcrunch.com/2016/08/04/strava-beacon)

### Sharing
- Logged-out (non-user) viewers of a public activity page see: activity type, distance, elevation, moving time, calories, photos, map, elevation profile, kudos count, comments, achievement count - but NOT exact start time or gear/bike details, and photos only show if the owner's profile is public. [Community: "How do your profile and activities appear when logged out?"](https://communityhub.strava.com/insider-journal-9/how-do-your-profile-and-activities-appear-when-logged-out-1525); [Activity Privacy Controls](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401987-activity-privacy-controls)
- Route sharing and "Generated Community Routes" let users publish routes drawn from aggregate athlete data for others to follow. [Generated Community Routes](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401756-generated-community-routes)

### Safety / privacy controls
- Three-tier activity visibility (Everyone / Followers / Only You) doubles as the leaderboard opt-out: setting an activity (or a blanket setting) to Followers/Only You removes it from public and segment leaderboards. Separate explicit checkboxes exist: "Show my activities on public leaderboards" and "Show my activities on segment leaderboards." [Activity Privacy Controls](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216919377-Activity-Privacy-Controls)
- Block/manage-followers tooling exists (Manage Followers and Block Athletes help page) though granular block/mute/report mechanics beyond that were not deeply covered in the sources returned - treat detail beyond "blocking exists" as unverified.
- No "quiet activity" terminology found in Strava's own docs (that phrasing may be a Garmin or other-app term) - unverified for Strava specifically.

### Cold start
- No official Strava documentation surfaced describing a deliberate "new athlete" cold-start flow (recommended clubs, seeded feed, etc.). The product mechanism that does the heavy lifting appears to be Clubs themselves - Strava's own marketing states clubs are the primary route to finding a training community ("why you need to join a club"), implying Strava leans on manual club discovery/joining rather than an algorithmic cold-start feed fill. [Strava Guide: Why You Need to Join a Club](https://stories.strava.com/articles/strava-guide-why-you-need-to-join-a-club) - inference labelled as such, not confirmed mechanism.

### Top complaints (Strava), each sourced
1. Rising subscription price (annual plan reported up from $59.99 to $79.99) with more features moved behind the paywall. [repreturn.com](https://repreturn.com/strava-review/)
2. Feed saturated with auto-posted, uncurated activities from watches/third-party integrations - "least social, social platform." [Medium](https://medium.com/@zolekker/strava-has-a-feed-problem-26a6d2a2f417)
3. Automated "motivational" kudos-style messages from Strava itself, widely called the most annoying app behaviour, not disable-able. [Community](https://communityhub.strava.com/strava-features-chat-5/how-to-remove-mute-motivational-messages-from-strava-502)
4. Challenge gallery perceived as brand advertising rather than organic community challenges. [Community](https://communityhub.strava.com/strava-features-chat-5/challenge-related-9330)
5. GPS/tracking glitches - inaccurate routes, bad auto-pause, inflated speed/distance, lost activities. [garagegymreviews.com](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/strava-fitness-app-review)
6. Data-usage/privacy concerns over broad rights claimed on user health data and photos. [repreturn.com](https://repreturn.com/strava-review/)
7. Weak customer support - reports of queries and complaints going unanswered, no visible support department. [repreturn.com](https://repreturn.com/strava-review/)
8. Difficulty cancelling subscriptions / unexpected charges. [repreturn.com](https://repreturn.com/strava-review/)
9. (Bonus, structural) Heatmap-driven location-pattern leakage never fully resolved since 2018, despite Privacy Zones. [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/strava-heatmaps-location-identity-doxxing-problem/)

### What Strava does exceptionally well (max 3)
- **Beacon**: a genuinely opt-in, no-account-needed, auto-expiring live-safety-share that solves a real solo-athlete safety problem without requiring the viewer to join the platform. [Coach](https://www.coachweb.com/fitness-apps/9051/strava-s-beacon-safety-feature-is-now-free-to-use)
- **Local Legend**: rewards *consistency/frequency* on a home segment rather than raw speed, giving ordinary local athletes (not just the fastest) a reason to keep showing up - a genuinely different growth-loop mechanic from a leaderboard. [DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2020/06/strava-legends-feature.html)
- **Verified Club badge**: an earned (non-purchasable), activity-gated (>=100 members, weekly posting) signal that lets real, active local communities stand out from dead/spam clubs without Strava having to hand-moderate every club. [Verified Badge for Clubs](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401828-verified-badge-for-clubs-on-strava)

### What's noise (max 3)
- Automated "motivational" kudos from Strava itself, undermining the authenticity of the kudos signal. [Community](https://communityhub.strava.com/strava-features-chat-5/how-to-remove-mute-motivational-messages-from-strava-502)
- Un-curated third-party-integration feed spam (every synced watch activity posted regardless of relevance). [Medium](https://medium.com/@zolekker/strava-has-a-feed-problem-26a6d2a2f417)
- Sponsor-driven challenge gallery that reads as ads, diluting the "challenge" concept. [Community](https://communityhub.strava.com/strava-features-chat-5/challenge-related-9330)

## GARMIN CONNECT

### Clubs / Groups
- Garmin's equivalent is "Groups": can be public or private, open or invite-only; each Group has a landing page showing members' recent activity, a bulletin/post feed, a leaderboard against group goals, an events calendar, shared routes, and local-segment fastest-times among group members. [gadgetsandwearables synthesis of leaked changes](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)
- As of 2026, Garmin's community layer is explicitly described as "deep in training and recovery but thinner on the social side" versus Strava's clubs/challenges/route-sharing, i.e. Groups exist but are a secondary feature relative to Garmin's training-plan core. [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)

### Follower system (2026 rebuild - flagged as leaked/rolling out, not fully confirmed GA)
- Garmin is replacing its older "Connections" (mutual, symmetric) model with a Strava-style asymmetric Followers system: follow requests, approval, limits, mutual-follow = "Friends," suggested users, and contact-based discovery. [garminrumors.com](https://garminrumors.com/garmin-connect-rolls-out-new-followers-system/); [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)
- Framed by industry press as a direct competitive response to Strava, leveraging Garmin's larger installed hardware base (~45M active users cited) as a data moat. [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)
- Privacy granularity: separate visibility controls per data type (profile, activities, steps, badges) with tiers "Only Me / My Followers / My Followers and Groups / Everyone" - finer-grained than Strava's flat 3-tier model. [gadgetsandwearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/05/21/garmin-connect-followers/)
- "Authorized Viewer": lets a user grant another person read-only access to their full account data (distinct from following) - resembles a caregiver/coach access model rather than a social one. [Garmin support - Managing Friends](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=ETHXgsWvaM4Zkyu30Uomj9)

### Local / privacy zones
- Garmin Connect has a distinct "Privacy Zone" concept (per Garmin's own support docs) though the specific mechanics were not deeply returned in this search - treat granular behaviour (radius, what's hidden) beyond "exists" as unverified pending direct doc read.
- No Garmin-specific equivalent of Strava's Heatmap/Local-Legend combination was found in these searches. Unverified whether Garmin has an aggregate heatmap feature at all.

### Live / LiveTrack (safety-adjacent, opt-in)
- LiveTrack shares an in-progress activity's live location, past locations, and path with contacts via a link (also postable to Facebook/Twitter historically). Requires explicit setup: Settings > Safety & Tracking > LiveTrack, and at least one contact must be configured before it can auto-start with an activity. [Garmin support](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=oPPijumqU23KHBCZk2wlc9); [Wareable](https://www.wareable.com/garmin/how-to-set-up-use-garmin-livetrack-safety-feature)
- A LiveTrack "Profile" dashboard lets the user configure privacy settings and a custom URL. [dcrainmaker Connect+ walkthrough](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2025/03/garmin-connect-plus-subscription-walkthrough.html)
- Distinct from Strava Beacon in framing: LiveTrack is presented more as a general trip-sharing/social broadcast feature (auto-post to social networks historically) rather than a dedicated stranger-safety product, though it's used for the same purpose in practice. [Coach](https://www.coachweb.com/gear/fitness-trackers/how-to-use-garmin-livetrack-and-share-your-location-with-friends)

### Challenges / badges
- Multi-month branded challenges (e.g. Jan-Mar 2026: 675km cycling, 300km running, 91mi walking) plus recurring weekly step challenges (compete to finish top-3). [blog.garminbadges.com](https://blog.garminbadges.com/2025/12/january-2026-challenges-and-badges.html)
- Group Challenges require being in a Group with other members first; created via a "+ Challenge" button on the web Challenge page. [Garmin forums](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-mobile-andriod/215737/how-to-create-group-challenge-on-garmin-connect)
- Badges (e.g. "60-Day Goal-Getter" for 60 consecutive days hitting a step goal) function as individual streak/consistency rewards, similar in spirit to Strava's Local Legend but tied to personal goals rather than a shared segment. [Garmin blog](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/blog/fitness/the-25-garmin-connect-badges-you-never-knew-you-needed/)
- User-requested but reportedly not yet standard: a **collective/cooperative summary** for group and individual challenges showing the combined total achievement of all participants (i.e. a co-op mode, not just a competitive leaderboard) - flagged in search synthesis as a current user ask. [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)

### Top complaints (Garmin Connect), each sourced
1. Social/community layer perceived as materially thinner than Strava's - "deep in training and recovery but thinner on the social side." [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)
2. Long-standing user complaints prompted the 2026 followers/authorized-viewer rebuild, implying the OLD "Connections" (symmetric-only, no approval tiers) model was seen as inflexible for years before being fixed. [gadgetsandwearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/05/21/garmin-connect-followers/)
3. Users want a cooperative/combined-total view for challenges; the competitive-only leaderboard framing was seen as a gap. [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)
4. Connect+ (paid subscription) launch triggered strong community backlash over paywalling and data-access concerns - "the Garmin subreddit and social media lit up." [Wareable](https://www.wareable.com/garmin/garmin-connect-plus-opinion-not-time-to-panic-yet)
5. General app-quality complaints: persistent SSO/login glitches driving users to look for alternatives. [Garmin forums thread](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-web/429419/fed-up-with-endless-sso-and-other-glitches-alternatives-to-garmin-connect)
6. UI/UX complaints specifically about Challenges and Notifications sections needing improvement (active user forum thread). [Garmin forums](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-mobile-ios/442756/garmin-connect-app-ui-ux-improvements-challenges-and-notifications/2057896)
7. A 2020 ransomware attack took the whole Garmin Connect service down - a reliability/trust scar referenced even in later coverage. [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/sanfrancisco/news/report-popular-garmin-fitness-tracking-service-goes-down-in-ransomware-attack) (older incident, included because it's still cited in trust discussions)
8. Data-privacy unease specifically around what's newly visible to followers (heart rate, location) once the more social model rolled out. [gadgetsandwearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/05/21/garmin-connect-followers/)

### What Garmin does exceptionally well (max 3)
- **Fine-grained visibility tiers** (Only Me / Followers / Followers+Groups / Everyone, set per data type - profile, activity, steps, badges) rather than one flat activity-level toggle. [gadgetsandwearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/05/21/garmin-connect-followers/)
- **Authorized Viewer** as a distinct concept from "follow" - purpose-built for a trusted read-only relationship (coach/carer/family), not just a lighter-weight follow. [Garmin support](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=ETHXgsWvaM4Zkyu30Uomj9)
- **Groups bundling** everything local-community-relevant (feed + leaderboard + events + routes + local segment times) into one page rather than scattering it across separate tabs. [the5krunner synthesis](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)

### What's noise (max 3)
- The 2026 social rebuild is explicitly copying Strava's follow/friend model, i.e. Garmin's own community had been asking for years for something it didn't have - the "noise" here is the long gap between user ask and product response.
- Cooperative/collective-challenge view still a gap as of this research, despite being a stated user want. [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)
- Connect+ paywall backlash suggests any monetisation layered onto social/training features draws immediate community suspicion - a caution more than a feature, but the negative signal itself is real "noise" in the ecosystem's trust. [Wareable](https://www.wareable.com/garmin/garmin-connect-plus-opinion-not-time-to-panic-yet)

## Lessons for a small strength network (max 15 bullets)

**Location privacy - adopt:**
1. Default location sharing to opt-IN, not opt-out, for anything showing "who else trained near you" - Strava's Flyby had to be flipped from opt-out to opt-in in Oct 2020 after real stalking/harassment risk; do not repeat the opt-out mistake even at launch. [DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2020/10/strava-flyby-feature.html)
2. Never build an aggregate heatmap/pattern-of-life feature without first asking what it reveals about the FEW users training somewhere unusual, not just the many training somewhere common - that inversion is exactly how Strava's 2018 incident happened. [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/strava-heatmaps-location-identity-doxxing-problem/)
3. Give users an independent "hide start/end point" toggle separate from any coarse-radius privacy zone - two different granularities of location risk need two different controls, per Strava's own design. [Edit Map Visibility](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000173384-Edit-Map-Visibility)
4. Any live-location-sharing safety feature (Volyume's answer to Beacon/LiveTrack) should require zero account from the viewer, auto-expire when the session ends, and cap the audience to a small named list - copy Beacon's shape, not LiveTrack's historic auto-post-to-social framing. [Coach](https://www.coachweb.com/fitness-apps/9051/strava-s-beacon-safety-feature-is-now-free-to-use)
5. Split visibility controls per data type (profile vs activity vs any bodyweight/measurement data) rather than one flat toggle - Garmin's per-field tiering is more privacy-respecting than Strava's single Everyone/Followers/Only-You switch, and maps well onto Volyume's existing ED-safety-driven data sensitivity (bodyweight is more sensitive than a session log). [gadgetsandwearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/05/21/garmin-connect-followers/)
6. Apply stricter defaults for under-18 accounts from day one (followers-only visibility, no leaderboard appearance, no unsolicited follow) rather than retrofitting it - Strava only added this in Sept 2021, years after launch. [Privacy defaults under 18](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412328250893-Your-Privacy-Control-Defaults-When-You-re-Under-18-on-Strava)

**Club/density mechanics - adopt or avoid:**
7. Adopt an earned (non-purchasable), activity-gated verification signal for clubs (Strava's 100-member + weekly-posting bar) so genuinely active local groups surface above dead ones - this directly solves "empty club" discovery without needing a human moderator to manually vet every club. [Verified Badge for Clubs](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401828-verified-badge-for-clubs-on-strava)
8. Adopt a consistency-based local-recognition mechanic (Strava's Local Legend logic - most efforts in a rolling window, not fastest time) as it rewards the exact behaviour a small, cold-start network needs most: people showing up repeatedly in the SAME place, which is what seeds visible local density. [DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2020/06/strava-legends-feature.html)
9. Avoid launching a public, sponsor-fillable challenge gallery - Strava's is widely perceived as ad space, not community; if Volyume runs challenges, keep them either founder-curated or purely peer/group-created, never open to paid placement.
10. Bundle a club's feed + leaderboard + events + shared routines into one page (Garmin's Groups pattern) rather than scattering across tabs - important for a small user base where a club might have single-digit members and can't sustain being spread across five under-populated tabs.
11. Give club admins real moderation power from day one (approve/remove members, promote/demote other admins) - Strava shipped this from early on and STILL has an open feature request for bulk removal; build bulk actions in from the start rather than retrofitting years later.

**Challenge mechanics that work small:**
12. Prefer cooperative/collective-total challenges (combined group progress toward one goal) over pure leaderboard competition for a small user base - Garmin users are explicitly asking for this because head-to-head leaderboards feel discouraging/pointless with few participants, which is exactly Volyume's cold-start condition. [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/05/01/garmin-vs-strava-connect/)
13. Keep group challenges private-by-default and participant-visible-only (Strava's model) rather than public, so a small group's low participation count is never publicly visible/embarrassing.
14. Treat automated/inauthentic engagement (Strava's platform-generated "motivational kudos") as an anti-pattern to avoid entirely - it was called out repeatedly as the single most annoying app behaviour and undermines trust in every other social signal on the platform.
15. Do not let feed content include everything auto-synced with no curation (Strava's top complaint) - for a small network the feed should default to human-authored moments (PRs, milestones, club posts) rather than posting every logged session, since low-volume feeds get noticed and low-quality noise is proportionally more damaging with fewer total posts to dilute it.
